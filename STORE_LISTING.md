### 3. Full Description *(Max 4,000 characters)*
```text
Miau CQ is the all-in-one companion app for amateur radio (Ham Radio) operators. Whether you are hunting rare DX stations from the field, participating in HF contests, or operating from your home shack, Miau CQ combines live cluster monitoring, logging, and USB CAT rig control in a single intuitive interface.

---

📡 REAL-TIME DX CLUSTER CLIENT
• Connect directly to worldwide DX Cluster nodes via Telnet.
• View incoming spots instantly with frequency, operating mode, callsign, spotter, and timestamps.
• Intelligent multi-band filtering: customize your feed for 160m, 80m, 40m, 30m, 20m, 17m, 15m, 12m, 10m, and 6m.
• Mode filters for CW (Morse Code), SSB (Phone), and DIGITAL (FT8 / FT4 / RTTY).
• Instant one-tap QRZ.com callsign lookup.

---

🎛️ USB CAT RIG CONTROL (YAESU FT-891 & MORE)
• Connect your Android device directly to your transceiver using a USB OTG cable and CAT interface (FTDI, CP210x, CH34x supported).
• Real-time dual VFO control (VFO-A and VFO-B) with direct frequency tuning.
• Change operating modes (USB, LSB, CW, CWR, AM, FM, DIG) directly from your screen.
• Tap any incoming DX cluster spot to instantly tune your transceiver frequency and mode.
• Built-in signal meter (S-Meter) and PTT transmit support.

---

📖 INTEGRATED QSO LOGBOOK
• Quickly log your two-way contacts with callsign, frequency, band, mode, signal reports (RST sent and received), and grid locator.
• Store time and date automatically in UTC.
• View and search past contacts by callsign or date.
• Keep your station records safe and organized on the go.

---

⚙️ SEAMLESS SERIAL PORT SETUP
• Automatic USB OTG serial converter detection.
• Configurable baud rates (4800, 9600, 19200, 38400 bps) and Yaesu-standard 2 stop bits.
• Hardware flow control (RTS/CTS handshake) support.

---

🔒 100% OPEN SOURCE & PRIVACY FOCUSED
• Free and open-source software on GitHub: https://github.com/dirtybug/MiauDX
• Upgraded for Android 16 (API 36) ensuring full security and compatibility with modern Android devices.

73 and Good DX!
```

### 3. Descrição Completa *(Máx 4.000 caracteres)*
```text
O Miau CQ é a aplicação completa para radioamadores! Combina monitorização em tempo real de DX Cluster, livro de registos de contactos (Logbook) e controlo de transceivers por cabo USB CAT numa interface intuitiva e moderna.

---

📡 DX CLUSTER EM TEMPO REAL
• Ligação direta a servidores DX Cluster mundiais via Telnet.
• Visualização imediata de spots com frequência, indicativo (callsign), modo, report e hora UTC.
• Filtros personalizados por bandas de HF (160m a 6m) e modos (CW, SSB, DIGITAL/FT8).
• Pesquisa rápida no QRZ.com com apenas um toque.

---

🎛️ CONTROLO CAT USB (YAESU FT-891 E OUTROS)
• Ligação direta ao rádio por cabo USB OTG com chips FTDI, CP210x ou CH34x.
• Sintonização de VFO-A e VFO-B diretamente no ecrã.
• Mudança rápida de modo (USB, LSB, CW, CWR, AM, FM, DIG).
• Sintonização imediata do rádio ao tocar em qualquer spot do DX Cluster.
• S-Meter visual e comando PTT.

---

📖 LIVRO DE REGISTOS (LOGBOOK)
• Registo rápido de QSOs com indicativo, frequência, modo, reports RST (enviado/recebido) e locator.
• Registo automático de data e hora em UTC.
• Consulta e pesquisa no histórico de contactos.

---

🔒 100% CÓDIGO ABERTO
• Desenvolvido para a comunidade de radioamadores.
• Código aberto no GitHub: https://github.com/dirtybug/MiauDX
• Compatível com Android 16 (API 36).

73 e bons contactos!
```
